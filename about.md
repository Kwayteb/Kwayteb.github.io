# About this community

This site is a place for clear, respectful writing about two legal traditions: Sharia (Islamic religious law) and formal state law. Articles here explain concepts, walk through specific rulings or statutes, and compare how each tradition handles a given question — written for readers without a legal or religious-studies background.

## Who this is for

Anyone curious about how law works, whether religious or secular, and open to reading about a tradition that isn't their own.
