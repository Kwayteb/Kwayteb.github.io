# Community guidelines

Because this site covers both religious and secular law, a few ground rules keep discussion useful for everyone.

1. **Explain, don't argue for a side.** Articles should help a reader understand a rule or ruling, not persuade them it's right or wrong.
2. **Cite your source.** Name the text, statute, school of thought, or case you're drawing from so readers can look further.
3. **Assume good faith.** Readers come from many backgrounds. Write as if a thoughtful stranger, religious or secular, familiar or not, is reading.
4. **Correct mistakes openly.** Law and religious interpretation are both contested; if a reader points out an error, fix it and note the correction.

*Edit this page to match how you actually want your community to run — these are a starting point, not a rulebook.*
